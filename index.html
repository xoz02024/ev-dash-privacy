Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

EV Dash & Engine Sound (“EV Dash”) displays dashboard information and generates simulated engine sounds using input from a compatible vehicle module or your phone. The app does not require an account.

Location and GPS

If you enable Phone GPS mode and allow precise location while using the app, EV Dash uses your device’s location while the app is in use to calculate speed for the dashboard and simulated sound. The app processes this information on your device and does not save a location history or automatically send location to the developer. You can turn off Phone GPS mode or revoke location access in iOS Settings.

Bluetooth and vehicle information

With Bluetooth permission, the app communicates directly with a compatible vehicle module and processes available vehicle readings and button input on your device for dashboard and sound features. Settings, button bindings and the identifier of a remembered module are stored on your device. You can revoke Bluetooth access in iOS Settings.

Diagnostics and sharing

Recent connection diagnostics and the latest location-mode status and speed are kept on the device. You can choose to copy and save or share a diagnostic snapshot. It can include displayed speed, vehicle readings, a connected device name or identifier, connection status and recent link logs. A copied snapshot is placed on your clipboard and saved locally. If you share a snapshot, the iOS share sheet lets you choose where it goes. The destination you select may retain a copy. Please review a snapshot before sharing it.

Purchases and Apple services

The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to offer the trial, a one-time purchase and purchase restoration. Apple processes transactions through the App Store. The app checks Apple-provided transaction information to determine access and stores certain trial status details in the device’s Keychain. The app does not automatically upload purchase records to a developer server. Apple handles its services under the Apple Privacy Policy. Depending on your device settings, Apple may also process diagnostics and usage information through its own services.

Other services

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not automatically send vehicle telemetry, location or diagnostic snapshots to the developer. This page contains no advertising or analytics code. GitHub Pages hosts this webpage. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Storage and deletion

App settings, a remembered module identifier, recent diagnostic information and any diagnostic snapshot you choose to save are stored locally. Deleting the app removes data in its app storage. Keychain entries, copies you have shared, backups and information held by Apple are managed separately. You can manage location and Bluetooth permissions in iOS Settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact xoz02024@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any diagnostics you choose to include. We use the information to respond to your inquiry. Copies may remain in email records.

Policy updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or its privacy practices. The date at the top indicates the latest update.
